Peer-to-peer: files stream device to device, end-to-end encrypted (WebRTC)
Works with the Floe web app and CLI: any two surfaces can transfer
Request links: make a one-time link so one person can send you files from their browser; nothing is saved until you accept, unless you turn on Auto-accept for that link
Request link limits: works only while Floe Desktop is open, awake and online; relayed drops stop at 2 GB; no resume; files are not scanned for malware
Every received file is checked against the SHA-256 its sender computed, when one is provided, and a file that does not match is deleted
Send folders without zipping
Paste to send: Ctrl+V a screenshot or files copied in Explorer
Local transfer history, stored on your device only
Notifications: turn them off or make them silent in Settings; Windows decides where they appear
Hide my IP: route the transfer through a relay so the other person never sees your IP
Self-hostable: point the app at your own server in Settings
