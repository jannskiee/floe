If someone sent you a request link, a link with `/r/` in it, you are the sender: open it in your browser,
pick files or folders, and click **Send**. Once they accept, the files go straight to the computer of
whoever made the link, not to a Floe server, and you need nothing but the browser. Request links are in
Beta.

<Info>
  During Beta, request links are supported in current Chrome and Edge on a computer, and the page says so
  under its heading. It does not turn other browsers away: it refuses only a browser that cannot open a
  WebRTC data channel, with "This browser cannot send through Floe".
</Info>

To send from a terminal instead, see [Send through a request link](/cli/send#send-through-a-request-link).
To make a link of your own, see [Request links](/desktop/request-links).

## The short version {#sending-through-a-request-link}

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the whole link">
    Open it in Chrome or Edge on a computer. Everything after the `#` is part of the link: a link cut short
    there shows "This link looks incomplete".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick files or folders">
    Drop them on the drop area, or use **Choose files** or **Choose folder**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Send">
    The button reads, for example, **Send 3 files**. Until this click, the page has contacted neither their
    computer nor Floe's signaling server.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for them to accept">
    The page reads "Waiting for them to accept" and counts down the minutes they have left to answer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Keep the tab in front until the files arrive">
    The page ends on, for example, **ALL 3 FILES ARRIVED**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Pick files {#pick-files}

Drop files or folders on the drop area, or use **Choose files** or **Choose folder**. A file let go anywhere
else on the page is not added. A drop can hold up to 10,000 files; for more, zip them first. Picking again
adds to the selection, and **Clear** empties it: there is no way to remove a single file. After the first
pick the drop area shrinks to an **Add more files** strip, which opens the file picker and still takes a
dropped folder; **Choose folder** comes back after **Clear**. For every limit on a drop, see
[Limits](/reference/limits#request-links).

Empty folders are not sent, and files arrive without their original modification times. A file nested very
deep, or with a very long name, can be too long for the other computer to save, so zip deeply nested folders
first. Where the browser cannot pick folders, **Choose folder** is missing and the page says "This browser
cannot pick folders. Drag the folder in, or zip it first."

**Hide my IP (relay only, 2 GB per drop)** sends everything through the relay, so the other side sees the
relay's address instead of yours. It is off each time the page opens, and it takes effect when you click
**Send**. Without it, the person who made the link can see your IP address once you click **Send**, before
they accept. See [What each side can see](#what-each-side-can-see).

## Send and wait for them to accept {#send-and-wait}

Click **Send**. The page reads "Connecting to their computer", then "Waiting for them to accept" with a
countdown, such as "They have 9 min to answer. Keep this page open." They have 9 minutes 45 seconds in all.
Nothing is written to their computer until they click **Accept**, unless they made the link with
[Auto-accept](/desktop/request-links#auto-accept) on, which lets an ordinary drop start without waiting for
them.

**Cancel** before they accept returns to your files, and you can send again. If they decline, or do not
answer in time, nothing is sent, and **Back to files** returns to your selection.

## Keep the page open {#keep-the-page-open}

The sending happens in your browser tab, so the tab has to stay open until the last file arrives. Keep the
tab in front. While the page connects, waits or sends, your browser asks before it closes or reloads the tab.
If you send from a phone or tablet anyway, keep the page open and the screen on.

Once a drop has been sending for 1 minute, the page estimates the time left for the whole drop and adds
advice:

| Time left | The page adds |
|---|---|
| More than 20 minutes | “Keep this computer plugged in and awake, and pin this tab.” |
| More than 2 hours | “If the connection drops, the file that was moving starts over.” |
| More than 24 hours | For example, "This drop would take about 26h 3m on this connection and will stop at 24 hours. Send fewer files." |

The lines add up: a drop with more than 2 hours left shows the first two.

There is no resume. If the connection drops, the files that arrived stay on their computer, and the rest,
including the file that was moving, has to be sent again through a new link.

If the browser discards the tab during a drop to save memory, the drop stops, and when you come back the
page reloads on **Connection lost** with how many files arrived, such as "4 of 12 files arrived. Ask them for
a new link to send the other 8." The tab keeps only those two numbers for this, never a file name, so the
page cannot list which files arrived.

## When the files arrive {#when-the-files-arrive}

Each file is listed under **ARRIVED** once their Floe has saved it under its final name. When every file
has arrived, the page says so, for example **ALL 12 FILES ARRIVED**, with the size, the time taken and the
route, direct or relay.

"Their app reports every file's SHA-256 matched." appears only when their Floe checked every file against
the SHA-256 your browser computed. See [File checks](/how-it-works/file-checks) for when that line is
missing.

**Cancel** after they accepted asks first: "Stop sending?". Files that already arrived stay on their
computer, and the link cannot be used again.

## Report a link {#report-a-link}

**Report this link**, at the bottom of the page until you click **Send**, starts an email carrying the
link's id: the part before the `#`. It never carries the part after it, so a report cannot be used to send
files through the link.

On floe.one the email goes to Floe's maintainers, at the address in
[SECURITY.md](https://github.com/jannskiee/floe/blob/main/SECURITY.md), the one security reports use. On a
self-hosted server it goes to the address the person who runs that server
[set for reports](/self-hosting/configuration#request-report-email). If they set none, the page shows no
**Report this link**.

## What the page shows {#what-the-page-shows}

| State | What you see | What you can do |
|---|---|---|
| Ready | “SEND FILES THROUGH THIS LINK” with a **Beta** tag, the drop area with **Choose files** and **Choose folder**, the Hide my IP check, and **Report this link**. After a pick: an **Add more files** strip in place of the drop area, the count, such as “3 FILES, 1.2 GB”, **Send 3 files** and **Clear**. | Pick files, then click **Send**. |
| Connecting | “Connecting to their computer” | Wait. **Cancel** returns to your files. |
| Waiting | “Waiting for them to accept”, the minutes they have left, and a **DIRECT** or **RELAY** badge | Keep the page open. **Cancel** returns to your files. |
| Sending | For example "SENDING 2 OF 12", the file's path, its progress, the speed and the time left, and the **ARRIVED** list | Keep the tab in front. **Cancel** asks first, then stops the drop. |
| Arrived | “ALL 12 FILES ARRIVED”, or “1 FILE ARRIVED”, then the size, the time taken and the route | Nothing more. The link is used up. |
| Stopped | One of the [messages](#messages) below. "Their computer is not connected right now", "Couldn't connect to their computer. Nothing was sent." and "Connection lost. Nothing was sent." offer **Try again**; a decline, a missed answer and "Relay limit" offer **Back to files**. | Follow the message. A card with no button stays until you reload the page, and after a reload you pick your files again. Ask for a new link only when the message says the link has ended or was already used. |

While the tab is hidden, its title shows the state instead, such as "Waiting for them to accept - Floe" or
"42% sent, 5 of 12 files - Floe".

## Messages {#messages}

A link that ended before anyone used it, because the person who made it clicked **Close link** or quit Floe,
or because its end time passed, says "This link has ended" for about 24 hours afterward. A link that was used,
or that was closed while a request was waiting for an answer or after a decline without being reopened,
says "This link has already been used" for about 24 hours after it closed. A server restart forgets both
sooner. After that, and for a link that never existed, the page reads "Their computer is not connected right
now", as it does while the computer that made the link is asleep or offline.

These are the messages of the web app at floe.one. A web app that someone hosts themselves can run an older
version, where a few of them read differently: see
[What an older web app shows](/self-hosting/operations#older-web-app-versions).

### Before and while you send {#messages-on-the-senders-page}

| Message | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| “Open in your browser”, with “Open the link in current Chrome or Edge on a computer.” | The link was opened inside another app's built-in browser, such as a chat app's. Nothing of the page loads until you continue. | Open the link in current Chrome or Edge on a computer. See [Open in your browser](/troubleshooting#open-in-your-browser). |
| “This link looks incomplete”, then “Copy the whole link again, including everything after the # sign.” | The link was cut short, often at the `#`. | Copy the whole link again from where you got it. |
| “This browser cannot send through Floe”, then “Open the link in current Chrome or Edge.” | This browser cannot open a WebRTC data channel, which is the one thing the page checks for. | Open the link in current Chrome or Edge. |
| “This browser cannot pick folders. Drag the folder in, or zip it first.” | This browser has no working folder picker, or a folder you dropped could not be read as one. | Do what the message says. |
| “Empty folders are not sent.” | A folder you dropped held an empty folder. The files are still selected. | Nothing. Zip the folder first if the empty folders matter. |
| “Relayed drops are capped at 2 GB. Turn off Hide my IP, or send 2 GB or less.” | With Hide my IP on, the drop goes through the relay, and your selection is over 2 GB. | Turn off Hide my IP, or click **Clear** and pick 2 GB or less. |
| “This drop has more than 10,000 files. Zip them first.” | A drop can hold up to 10,000 files. Nothing from that pick was added. | Zip them and send the archive. |
| “A folder path is too long to send. Zip deeply nested folders first.” | A file sits too deep in its folders, or its name is too long, to be sent. Nothing from that pick was added. | Do what the message says. |
| “Some files in this folder could not be read. Nothing was added.” | The browser could not read part of a folder you dropped, so none of it was added. | Check the folder, or zip it and send the archive. |
| “Their computer is not connected right now”, then “They may have closed Floe. Your files stay selected.” | Floe cannot reach the computer that made the link: it is asleep or offline, or Floe there stopped without closing the link. It also covers a link that never existed, or that ended more than about a day ago. | Click **Try again** once they have Floe open, or ask for a new link. |
| “This link has ended”, then “Ask them for a new link.” | The link was closed, or reached its end time, before anyone used it. There is no **Try again**. | Ask them for a new link. |
| “This link has already been used”, then “Ask for a new link.” | The link took its one drop, someone else is connected through it now, the person who made it declined a request and has not reopened the link, or they closed the link while a request was waiting for their answer or after a decline without reopening it. The server also gives this answer for a link that senders have joined more than 6 times in 1 minute. | Ask the person who made it for a new link. If you had just tried several times in a row, wait 1 minute and reload the page first. |
| “Request links are turned off right now” | The server has turned request links off. A link that was waiting for a sender when that happened has ended. | Try again later. If the page then says "This link has ended", ask for a new link. |
| “Request links are not available on this Floe server.” | The server did not answer the page within 10 seconds, as a server older than request links does not. | Ask the person who made the link. |
| “Couldn't connect to their computer. Nothing was sent.” | Your browser and their PC could not finish connecting. | Click **Try again**. If it keeps happening, a network on one side may be blocking the connection. |
| “This Floe server has no relay right now. Turn off Hide my IP to send directly.” | Hide my IP needs a relay, and the server's list has none. | Turn off Hide my IP to send directly. |
| “Couldn't check for a relay. Try again, or turn off Hide my IP to send directly.” | Hide my IP needs a relay, and the page could not read the server's relay list: the request failed, or the server was limiting requests from your network. | Click **Send** again: your files stay selected, and the page asks the server again on every try. If the message comes back, wait 1 minute and try once more, or turn off Hide my IP to send directly. |
| “Too many connections from this network. Trying again shortly.” | The server is limiting connections from your network for a moment. | Wait: the page tries again by itself. |
| “They declined. Nothing was sent.” | They clicked **Decline**. | Ask them before you send again. |
| “They did not answer in time. Nothing was sent.” | Nobody clicked **Accept** or **Decline** in time. The link stays open. | Ask them to be at their PC, then send again. |
| “Relay limit”, then “This connection needs a relay, and relayed drops are capped at 2 GB. Send 2 GB or less, or try another network.” | The connection goes through the relay, because the two networks allow nothing else or because their Floe has Hide my IP on, and your selection is over 2 GB. Nothing was sent. | Click **Back to files**, then **Clear**, and pick 2 GB or less. Or try another network, or ask them to turn off Hide my IP if it is on. See [File size limits](/how-it-works/2gb-limit). |
| “This computer may have slept. If the connection drops, the file that was moving starts over.” | While you were away from the tab, the computer's clock jumped ahead of the page's, as it does after a sleep. | Keep the computer awake until the last file arrives. |
| “You stopped this drop.” | You clicked **Cancel** after they accepted, and confirmed. | Ask for a new link to send the rest. |
| “Their Floe needs an update.” | Their Floe and this page speak versions of the transfer protocol that do not overlap. | Ask them to update Floe. |
| “Connection lost”, then for example “4 of 12 files arrived. Ask them for a new link to send the other 8.” | The connection dropped after they accepted, or the browser discarded the tab during the drop. There is no resume. | Do what the message says. |
| “Connection lost. Nothing was sent.” | The connection dropped before they accepted. | Click **Try again**. |
| “Could not read a file”, then the file's path and “may have been moved or renamed, or its drive disconnected.” | A file you picked could not be read when its turn came. On Windows that includes a file whose full path is over 260 characters, which Chrome and Edge cannot read. | Check the file, then ask for a new link to send the rest. |

### When their Floe stops the drop {#when-their-floe-stops-the-drop}

The page shows one of these, then how many files were saved, such as "4 of 12 files were saved." ("1 of 1
file was saved." for a single file) or "Nothing was sent.", and the files that arrived under **ARRIVED**:

| Message | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| “Their computer ran out of space” | Their save drive filled up, or the next file would have left it with less than the 2 GB it has to keep free. | Ask them to free space, then ask for a new link. |
| “Their computer could not save a file” | Windows refused a write on their side. | Ask them to check their save folder. |
| “A file changed or was damaged on the way, so their Floe deleted it”, and after the count “Ask them for a new link to send the rest.” | A file's SHA-256 did not match on their side. | Do what the message says. |
| “Relayed drops are capped at 2 GB”, with “Nothing was sent. Send 2 GB or less.” in place of the count when nothing was saved | Their Floe found the drop going through the relay and past 2 GB. | Send 2 GB or less, or ask them to turn off Hide my IP. |
| “A folder path is too long for their computer”, and after the count “Zip deeply nested folders first.” | A file's path was too deep or too long to save on their side. | Do what the message says. |
| “A file is too large for the drive they save to” | Their save drive cannot hold a file that large. | Ask them to save to another drive. |
| “Their computer blocked saving a file” | Their Floe could not move a finished file into place, after 5 minutes of retries. The complete file stays there with a `.part` ending. | Ask them about that file before you send it again. |
| “More data arrived than they accepted”, and after the count “If files changed after you chose them, ask them for a new link.” | Your browser sent more than the count and size they accepted. | Do what the message says. |
| “They stopped this drop.” | They clicked **Cancel drop**, or closed the link or quit Floe while your request was waiting. | Ask them before you send again. |
| “This drop reached the 24-hour limit” | A single drop stops at 24 hours. | Ask for a new link and send fewer files. |
| “The drop stopped on their computer.” | Their Floe stopped the drop for another reason. | Ask them what happened. |

## If something goes wrong {#if-something-goes-wrong}

- "Their computer is not connected right now": Floe has to be open on their PC, awake and online. See
  [Troubleshooting](/troubleshooting#their-computer-is-not-connected-right-now).
- "This link has already been used": a link takes one drop, so ask for a new one. See
  [Troubleshooting](/troubleshooting#this-link-has-already-been-used).
- "They did not answer in time. Nothing was sent.": they have 9 minutes 45 seconds to answer. See
  [Troubleshooting](/troubleshooting#they-did-not-answer-in-time).
